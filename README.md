## 📖 Overview

Most wildlife field guides and culinary seafood resources treat nature and nutrition as two separate worlds—showing either a wild creature in its habitat *or* a fish fillet on a plate. **Fauna & Seafood World** unites both perspectives for **students** and **adults** by pairing every single species with **two synchronized visuals**:

1. **Left Visual (`🐋 Living Species in Nature`):** How the mammal, bird, ocean fish, freshwater fish, shellfish, reptile, amphibian, or pollinator looks in its natural wild biome.
2. **Right Visual (`🍣 Seafood Harvest / Habitat & Ecological Role`):** Its culinary seafood form, nesting or pollination behavior, or ecological food-web function.

Engineered as a self-contained, single-file web application (`index.html`), it runs offline-ready on mobile phones, tablets, and classroom desktops with zero build steps, zero logins, and zero external dependencies.

## ✨ Complete Feature Breakdown

### 🖼️ 1. Dual-Visual Discovery Engine (450 Paired Visuals)
* **Multi-Stage Wikimedia & Wikipedia Media Pipeline:** Automatically resolves distinct, non-duplicate real photography for both the living species and its seafood harvest or habitat role, backed by `localStorage` caching (`v5`) and concurrency throttling.
* **Data Saver & Offline Vector Mode (`📶`):** One-click toggle between **Real Dual Photos** and zero-bandwidth **Zoology Vector Diagrams** for offline study or slow mobile data.
* **Side-by-Side Comparison Tray (`⚖️`):** Pin any two species (e.g., *Rohu vs. Catla*, *Alligator vs. Gharial*, or *Honeybee vs. Bumblebee*) to compare taxonomy, habitats, nutrition, and behaviors in a split modal.
* **Instant Multi-Field Search & Revision List (`★`):** Search simultaneously across English names, Hindi names, Spanish names, Latin scientific names, Omega-3s, habitats, and dishes—and star any specimen to build a personal **Revision List**.

### 🔊 2. 10-Language Pronunciation & Full-Lesson Audio
* **Web Speech API Integration:** Native voice pronunciation in **English (US, UK, India), Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, and Japanese**.
* **Dual-Speed Controls & Full Narrator:** Every card includes **🐢 Slow (0.56×)** for syllable-by-syllable clarity and **⚡ Fast (1.02×)** for natural speech, plus a **🔊 Read Full Lesson Aloud** button inside every detail modal.
* **Interactive Spelling & Cue Cards:** Clicking any card opens letter-by-letter spelling tiles, phonetic syllable breakdowns, zoology memory clues, and mastery toggles (`✓ Mastered`).

### 🧪 3. Daily Habits, Gamified Labs & Assessment Arena
* **🔥 Daily 5-Species Lesson & Streak Tracker:** Automatically curates 5 fresh specimens each day and tracks consecutive learning days.
* **🌊 Interactive Ecosystem & Seafood Nutrition Lab:** Complete guided missions (*Omega-3 & Brain-Healthy Seafood Plate*, *Keystone Pollinators & Seed Planters*, *Apex Predators & Ecosystem Engineers*) with **live progress meters** and per-species feedback.
* **🃏 Spaced-Repetition Flashcard Deck:** Flip-card practice deck prioritizing starred revision items with one-tap mastery tracking (`✓ Got It!` vs. `🔁 Need Practice`).
* **♾️ Endless 3-Hearts Survival Arena:** High-stakes identification streak challenge with persistent all-time high-score tracking.
* **🎯 6-Mode Quiz Arena & Canvas Certificate Generator:**
  1. *Wildlife & Seafood Detective*
  2. *Listen & Spell*
  3. *Culinary & Behavior Match*
  4. *Ecology & Seafood Nutrition*
  5. *World Biome Geography*
  6. *Grand Zoologist Mix*
  * Scoring **≥ 70%** unlocks a custom-rendered HTML5 Canvas **Fauna & Seafood Mastery Certificate** downloadable as a high-resolution `.png` or printable landscape `.pdf`.

### 🗺️ 4. World Biome & Ocean Map, Accessibility & Portable Progress
* **Interactive SVG World Biome & Ocean Map (`🗺️`):** Filter all 225 species across 6 global biomes and ocean basins (*Open Oceans & Coral Reefs*, *Rivers, Lakes & Wetlands*, *Polar & Subarctic Seas*, *African Savannas & Deserts*, *Tropical Rainforests & Jungles*, and *Temperate Forests & Mountains*).
* **Inclusive Study Controls:** Includes **Student Mode**, **Memory Hide** (blurs names until hover for active recall), **Dark Mode (`🌙`)**, **High-Legibility Dyslexia Spacing (`Aa`)**, **Print-Optimized 2-Column Study Sheets (`🖨️`)**, and **Save / Load Progress Codes (`💾 / 📂`)**.
